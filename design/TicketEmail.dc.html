Hi Olivia,
Thanks for the details. A drop in suction after a few weeks is almost always a clogged filter rather than a fault, so let's try this first:
1.
Twist the dust cup off and pull the HEPA filter straight out.
2.
Tap out the dust, rinse under cold water, and let it dry for 24 hours.
3.
Check that the rubber seal on the cup sits flat, then reassemble.
