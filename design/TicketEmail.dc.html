Hi Olivia,

Thanks for the details. A drop in suction after a few weeks is almost always a clogged filter rather than a fault, so let's try this first:
图示：拆下并清洗 HEPA 滤芯
素材库 IMG-V2-014
1. Twist the dust cup off and pull the HEPA filter straight out.
2. Tap out the dust, rinse under cold water, and let it dry for 24 hours.
3. Check that the rubber seal on the cup sits flat, then reassemble.
V2 filter care guide
forcome.com/support/v2-filter · 白名单域名
If suction is still weak after the filter is fully dry, reply to this email and we'll send a replacement filter under warranty.
